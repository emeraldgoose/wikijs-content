# Eval-driven development: Lessons from evaluating GenAI at scale

> **Level**: Intermediate  
> **Source**: [Eval-driven development: Lessons from evaluating GenAI at scale](#)  
> **Last Updated**: 2026-08-28

## Introduction

Eval-driven development is a software engineering methodology that prioritizes evaluation as a foundational discipline to address the non-deterministic and subjective nature of Generative AI outputs. This approach is necessary because traditional testing assumptions often fail with Large Language Models, which involve complex workflows requiring cascading reasoning and tool calls. Key use cases include building trustworthy AI-powered features such as automated review highlights, customer support agents, and smart communication tools, as well as internal product analysis. By establishing robust evaluation infrastructure, organizations can systematically manage failure modes and ensure product reliability when implementing Generative AI at scale.

## Core Concepts

### Evaluation as a First-Class Engineering Discipline
The article argues that evaluation should not be an afterthought or a manual testing step end-of-cycle, but a core part of the development lifecycle.
*   **Shift in Mindset:** Teams must treat evaluation infrastructure with the same rigor as application code.
*   **Early Planning:** Evaluation strategy should be defined at the outset of a project to avoid technical debt and ad-hoc fixes later.
*   **Integration:** Evaluations are integrated into the CI/CD pipeline to ensure quality gates are consistent and automated.

### Adapting to Non-Determinism and Subjectivity
Unlike traditional software where outputs are deterministic and "correctness" is binary, Generative AI requires a new evaluation framework.
*   **Subjective Metrics:** Success is often measured by quality, tone, or helpfulness rather than a simple true/false logic check.
*   **Probabilistic Outputs:** Engineers must account for the fact that prompting the same model twice can yield different results, requiring statistical analysis rather than single-run tests.
*   **LLM-as-a-Judge:** Due to the high level of judgment required, AI models are often used to evaluate other AI models, though this introduces its own failure modes that must be managed.

### Decomposing Complex AI Workflows
LLM interactions often involve multiple steps (retrieval, reasoning, tool calls, generation), each of which can fail independently.
*   **Granular Testing:** Instead of evaluating only the final output, teams break down the chain to evaluate specific components (e.g., validating retrieved context separately from the generated response).
*   **Isolation of Failure Modes:** This decomposition helps pinpoint whether an error occurred during data retrieval, logic reasoning, or text generation.
*   **Chain Reliability:** Ensuring the entire pipeline is robust requires understanding how different parts of the system interact and fail.

### Centralized Infrastructure and Tooling
Airbnb employs a model where a central infrastructure team supports product teams with shared best practices.
*   **Shared Foundations:** An infrastructure team provides standardized tooling so product teams do not have to reinvent evaluation frameworks for every feature.
*   **Cross-Domain Learnings:** Successes and failures in one domain (e.g., customer support) can inform evaluation strategies in another (e.g., smart communication).
*   **Scalability:** Centralized tooling allows for running evaluations at scale across the product suite rather than in isolation.

### Flexible, Context-Specific Criteria
There is no "one-size-fits-all" approach to evaluating GenAI; criteria must match the specific product use case.
*   **Product-Specific Evals:** Each team defines their own evaluation criteria based on their specific user needs (e.g., accuracy for support vs. tone for marketing).
*   **Iterative Process:** Evaluation protocols evolve as the product matures and as new failure modes are discovered.
*   **Human-in-the-Loop:** While automation is key, human oversight remains necessary to validate subjective quality and catch edge cases AI judges might miss.
