# Eval-driven development for Generative AI

> **Level**: Intermediate  
> **Source**: [Eval-driven development: Lessons from evaluating GenAI at scale](#)  
> **Last Updated**: 2026-08-29

## Introduction

Eval-driven development is a software engineering methodology that treats rigorous evaluation as a first-class discipline rather than an afterthought in the development of Generative AI systems. This methodology addresses the non-deterministic and subjective nature of large language model outputs, which complicates traditional testing and introduces unique failure modes within complex reasoning chains. By establishing common foundations and infrastructure for assessment, teams can ensure the reliability and trustworthiness of AI-powered features. Key use cases include deploying AI customer support agents, generating content summaries, and enabling smart communication tools, where consistent and accurate performance is required at scale.

## Core Concepts

### Concept 1: Evaluation as a First-Class Engineering Discipline
To build trustworthy Generative AI products, evaluation must be integrated into the development process from the beginning rather than treated as a secondary step.
- **Strategic Planning:** Evaluation work is challenging and must be planned for "at the outset" rather than added as an afterthought.
- **Engineering Mindset:** Airbnb treats evaluation not as a QA checkbox but as a core engineering discipline essential for product reliability.
- **Trust Building:** This approach is foundational to scaling GenAI features, such as AI customer support and smart communication, safely across the product.

### Concept 2: Unique Challenges of GenAI Testing
Evaluating LLM-based systems breaks traditional software testing assumptions, requiring new methods and acknowledging specific points of failure.
- **Non-Determinism:** Unlike traditional software, LLM outputs are not deterministic; the same input can yield different results.
- **Subjectivity:** There is often no single objective definition of "correct," requiring significant human or AI judgment.
- **Complex Chaining:** A single interaction may involve multiple independent failure points across retrieval, reasoning, tool calls, and generation.
- **AI-evaluating-AI:** Due to the need for judgment, AI is often required to evaluate AI, which introduces its own potential failure modes.

### Concept 3: Centralized Infrastructure with Decentralized Implementation
Airbnb employs a hybrid model where an infrastructure team provides shared tools while allowing product teams flexibility to define their own needs.
- **Shared Foundations:** An infrastructure team provides tooling, best practices, and common principles incorporated from learnings across domains.
- **Team Autonomy:** Individual product teams retain ownership over their specific evaluation criteria, processes, and workflows.
- **No One-Size-Fits-All:** Recommendations are not prescriptive; different approaches are accepted based on the specific needs of the product being built.
- **Cross-Domain Learning:** Insights and tooling are shared across the organization to prevent siloed efforts and elevate the standard for all teams building GenAI.
