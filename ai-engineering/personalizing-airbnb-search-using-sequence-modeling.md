# Personalizing Airbnb search using sequence modeling

> **Level**: Advanced  
> **Source**: [Personalizing Airbnb search by learning from the guest journey](#)  
> **Last Updated**: 2026-08-29

## Introduction

Personalizing Airbnb search using sequence modeling refers to the application of Transformer-based architectures to encode longitudinal guest behavior data for search result optimization. This methodology addresses the scalability and expressiveness limitations of prior hand-crafted feature systems by analyzing complex behavioral sequences, including views, bookings, and cancellations accumulated over years. By modeling the full guest journey, the system generates richer user preference representations to surface relevant listings during multi-session trip planning. This approach enhances ranking accuracy and system maintainability within large-scale hospitality recommendation engines.

## Core Concepts

### Concept 1: The Guest Journey as Long-Term Sequential Data
*   **Multi-Session Planning:** Trip planning on Airbnb rarely occurs in a single session; guests often browse dozens of listings over several days.
*   **Historical Accumulation:** A guest's behavior accumulates over years, including a history of views, past bookings, reviews, and cancellations.
*   **Preference Signals:** Taken together, these temporal events reveal a comprehensive profile of what a guest values, beyond what a single search query can express.

### Concept 2: Shift from Static Aggregation to Sequence Modeling
*   **Legacy Approach:** Previous search ranking relied on hand-crafted features, such as aggregated statistics (e.g., total past bookings, average listing price).
*   **Limitations of Legacy System:** As feature counts grew into the hundreds, the manual approach became difficult to scale and lacked expressive power.
*   **New Approach:** The system replaces static statistics with a sequence modeling framework that processes the chronological order of guest events to capture dynamic preferences.

### Concept 3: Transformer-Based Architecture for Representation Learning
*   **Deep Learning Model:** Airbnb implemented a Transformer-based sequence model to handle the complexity of the guest journey.
*   **Rich Representations:** The model encodes years of behavior to create dense, richer representations of guest preferences compared to simple statistical features.
*   **Personalization Goal:** The objective is to use these learned representations to surface the most relevant listings at the right time during a user's trip planning process.

### Concept 4: Challenges in Event Imbalance and Optimization
*   **Data Volume:** Event sequences are dominated by listing views, with some guests accumulating hundreds of thousands of them, making raw sequences computationally intractable.
*   **Signal vs. Noise:** Unlike social media platforms that optimize for engagement (views), Airbnb optimizes for booking conversion.
*   **Event Deliberateness:** Bookings are rare and deliberate actions, whereas listing views may reflect casual browsing, creating a mismatch between data volume and business intent.
