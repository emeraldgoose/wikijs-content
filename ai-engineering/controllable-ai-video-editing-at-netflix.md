# Controllable AI Video Editing at Netflix

> **Level**: Intermediate

> **Last Updated**: 2026-08-28

## Introduction

Controllable AI Video Editing at Netflix is an internal research initiative developing artificial intelligence tools designed to enable precise, selective modifications to video footage while maintaining source integrity. This approach addresses limitations in existing generative models that often alter unintended elements, ensuring technology supports creative visions without compromising physical continuity. Primary use cases involve streamlining the production of promotional assets, such as trailers and social media clips, by facilitating complex edits like adding visual elements, patching backgrounds, or removing unwanted objects. By reducing reliance on extensive manual editing, the research aims to empower artists to dictate specific changes within the video production workflow.

## Core Concepts

### Concept 1: Creator-Centric AI Integration
The research emphasizes that AI tools must be built to serve the specific creative intentions of human artists rather than replacing their decision-making.
*   **Serving Creative Vision:** Technology is developed to help storytellers bring their creative visions to life, ensuring AI acts as a supportive feature rather than an autonomous agent.
*   **Protecting Creative Choice:** The research principle is to expand creative options through AI while keeping artists in precise control of the final editorial vision.
*   **Closed Collaboration:** Tools are designed based on close collaboration with the teams crafting assets to identify and fill specific gaps in current technology.

### Concept 2: Maintaining Source Footage Integrity
A key challenge identified is preventing AI from altering elements of a scene that should remain untouched during the editing process.
*   **Avoiding Full Regeneration:** Unlike many generative models that regenerate every pixel, Netflix’s approach seeks methods that do not alter the entire frame when making a specific edit.
*   **Isolating Changes:** Effective tools must be able to isolate specific edits (such as removing an object) without inadvertently changing unrelated visual elements.
*   **Preserving Physical Continuity:** Edits must seamlessly integrate new elements or patches without breaking the physical logic or continuity of the original scene.

### Concept 3: Streamlining Complex Manual Workflows
The research focuses on automating labor-intensive tasks involved in creating promotional assets to improve efficiency for professional editors.
*   **Reducing Manual Hours:** The goal is to transform raw footage into polished assets without requiring hours of specialized manual editing work.
*   **Targeted Task Automation:** Specific complex edits targeted include adding new visual elements, patching or replacing backgrounds, and removing unwanted objects.
*   **Promotional Asset Production:** The technology is specifically aimed at supporting the creation of trailers, teasers, and social short-form videos that elevate original footage.
