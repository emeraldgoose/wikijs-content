# Controllable AI Video Editing

> **Level**: Intermediate  
> **Source**: [Toward More Controllable AI Video Editing: An Early Research Exploration at Netflix](#)  
> **Last Updated**: 2026-08-28

## Introduction

Controllable AI video editing refers to generative video manipulation techniques designed to allow creators to precisely dictate specific changes within a scene while preserving the integrity of the original footage and physical continuity. This approach addresses significant limitations in current tools, which often fail to isolate edits or require extensive manual labor to maintain scene coherence during production. By enabling targeted modifications, the technology streamlines the creation of promotional assets like trailers and social media clips. Key use cases include seamlessly adding visual elements, patching or replacing backgrounds, and removing unwanted objects. Early industry research, such as initiatives by Netflix, focuses on ensuring AI serves creative visions by confining edits to specific areas without altering untouched source material.

## Core Concepts

### Concept 1
**Precise Element Isolation**
*   Enables artists to dictate exactly what changes occur within a video clip and how those changes are executed.
*   Focuses on editing specific visual elements rather than regenerating every pixel in the frame.
*   Prevents the system from inadvertently altering elements that were intended to remain untouched during the editing process.

### Concept 2
**Preservation of Source Footage Integrity**
*   Prioritizes maintaining the original quality and data of the raw footage alongside any applied edits.
*   Addresses the challenge of "unintended edits" where current generative models fail to preserve the source material's stability.
*   Ensures that the final polished asset elevates the original footage without degrading the elements that define the scene.

### Concept 3
**Alignment with Creative Intent**
*   Ensures that AI technology is deliberately applied to serve the creative vision of the storyteller and artists.
*   Keeps artists in precise control of their final vision rather than relying on fully automated generation.
*   Aims to protect and expand creative choice, making the technology a tool for empowerment rather than a replacement for human decision-making.

### Concept 4
**Maintenance of Physical Continuity**
*   Requires edits (such as adding new elements or removing objects) to adhere to the physical logic of the scene.
*   Prevents the breaking of scene continuity when performing complex tasks like patching backgrounds or seamlessly adding visual elements.
*   Ensures that promotional assets, such as trailers and teasers, remain visually coherent with the original footage.
