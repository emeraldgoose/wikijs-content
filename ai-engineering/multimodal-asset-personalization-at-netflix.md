# Multimodal Asset Personalization at Netflix

> **Level**: Intermediate  
> **Source**: [MAPS: Netflix’s Multimodal Asset Personalization at Scale](#)  
> **Last Updated**: 2026-08-28

## Introduction

Multimodal Asset Personalization at Netflix (MAPS) is a recommendation system utilizing multimodal embeddings to match members with personalized static artwork and video previews based on content features rather than historical interaction metrics. This approach resolves the cold-start problem encountered during new title launches by enabling immediate personalization without reliance on existing engagement data or popularity heuristics. By interpreting visual and auditory elements directly, the framework allows recommendation models to tailor the interface even when interaction history is insufficient. Key use cases include optimizing thumbnail selection and autoplay video previews to facilitate content discovery. Consequently, the system ensures relevant visual cues are presented from the moment a title debuts, enhancing the discovery journey regardless of data maturity.

## Core Concepts

### Concept 1: Visual Asset Personalization
*   **Definition of Assets:** In the Netflix context, "assets" refer to visual cues designed to connect members with stories they will love.
*   **Types of Assets:** These include the static artwork displayed on a title and the video previews that autoplay while browsing.
*   **Personalization Goal:** The core objective is to select the specific image or video preview (e.g., of *Squid Game*) that is most relevant to each individual member.

### Concept 2: The Cold-Start Problem with Opaque Models
*   **Limitation of Previous Models:** Older models treated every asset as an opaque ID, meaning they could not understand the actual content within the artwork or video.
*   **Data Dependency:** Because assets were viewed as distinct IDs, personalization could only occur after enough interaction data had accumulated for that specific asset.
*   **New Title Challenges:** Right after a title launches, there is insufficient interaction history. Previously, this required relying on exploration strategies or popularity heuristics, which ignored individual member tastes.

### Concept 3: Multimodal Embeddings for Content Understanding
*   **Enhanced Perception:** Multimodal embeddings allow the recommendation models to "see and hear" the assets they recommend, rather than treating them as blind IDs.
*   **Immediate Signal Transfer:** When a new asset arrives with its embedding, the model can immediately recognize it and transfer member taste signals from related assets.
*   **Reduced Data Requirement:** This capability enables personalization to begin much sooner—close to a title’s launch—without waiting for a large volume of interaction history.

### Concept 4: Production Systems and Integration Strategy
*   **Key Systems:** The technology is applied across three specific production systems: artwork personalization, query-aware artwork ranking, and video preview personalization.
*   **Integration Tactic:** Before committing to full end-to-end integration and A/B testing, the team employs a "cheap trick" for selecting new embeddings to ensure validity and efficiency.
*   **Scalability:** The approach is designed to function at scale, addressing the disparity between how well models answered asset selection for existing libraries versus new launches.
