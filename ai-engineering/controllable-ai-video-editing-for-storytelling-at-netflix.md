# Controllable AI Video Editing for Storytelling at Netflix

> **Level**: Intermediate  
> **Source**: [Toward More Controllable AI Video Editing: An Early Research Exploration at Netflix](#)  
> **Last Updated**: 2026-08-28

## Introduction

Controllable AI Video Editing for Storytelling at Netflix is a research initiative designed to enable precise, artist-directed video modifications while preserving source footage integrity. This technology addresses inefficiencies in manual editing and limitations in generative models that often compromise untargeted elements during complex transformations. It supports the production of promotional assets, such as trailers, teasers, and social short-form videos, where maintaining narrative and physical continuity is essential. Key use cases encompass seamlessly adding visual elements, patching backgrounds, and removing unwanted objects without disrupting scene consistency. Ultimately, the framework ensures artificial intelligence augments creative vision by allowing users to dictate specific changes rather than regenerating entire frames indiscriminately.

## Core Concepts

### Concept 1: The Demand for Complex Promotional Asset Creation
Netflix utilizes technology to connect stories with global audiences, relying heavily on the creation of promotional assets such as trailers, teasers, and social short-form videos. These assets must elevate the original footage while maintaining narrative consistency.
*   **Manual Workload:** Transforming raw footage into a polished final asset typically requires hours of specialized manual editing work.
*   **Complex Requirements:** Editors often need to perform difficult tasks such as seamlessly adding new visual elements, patching or replacing backgrounds, or removing unwanted objects.
*   **Physical Continuity:** Edits must be executed without breaking the scene’s physical continuity, ensuring the promotional material remains believable and high-quality.

### Concept 2: Limitations of Current Generative Video Models
While recent advancements in AI show promise for streamlining editing workflows, existing generative models often fail to meet professional standards required for storytelling.
*   **Inability to Isolate Changes:** Many current methods regenerate every pixel in the video to make an edit, rather than targeting specific regions.
*   **Loss of Source Integrity:** This full-pixel regeneration fails to isolate changes and inadvertently alters elements that should remain untouched.
*   **Unintended Edits:** The inability to preserve the integrity of source footage leads to unintended edits that disrupt the scene, preventing reliable professional use.

### Concept 3: The Principle of Controllable and Intent-Driven AI
The research goal is defined not just by automation, but by ensuring AI serves the creative intent of the artist rather than replacing human judgment.
*   **Creative Intent First:** Technology must be applied deliberately to ensure it always serves the creative vision of the storyteller.
*   **Preservation of Choice:** AI tools should protect and expand creative choice, rather than restricting artists to the outputs generated by a model.
*   **Precise Control:** Artists must be kept in precise control of their final vision, empowering them to dictate exactly what changes in the video and how those changes occur.
